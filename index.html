WEB ACCESSIBILTY

Building accessible websites is a top priority, regardless of a user's abilities or accessibility requirements. Individuals in this category may have problems with their hands, vision, or hearing. Websites that are accessible allow all users, including those with disabilities or who rely on assistive technologies like screen readers, to easily navigate and engage with the content. Web accessibility is crucial because it ensures that everyone can use the web fairly. This facilitates access to services and information for those with disabilities and is mandated by law in numerous jurisdictions. More people will visit websites that are easy to use even if they have a disability, such as a broken arm. In order to be accessible, websites must adhere to certain standards. For instance, the site's navigation should be intuitive and all content should be viewable, audible, or otherwise understandable. Creating user-friendly websites is not only the moral thing to do, but also benefits everyone. Consider the user experience of all visitors when launching a website.
