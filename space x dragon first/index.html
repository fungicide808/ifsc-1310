First Private Spacecraft to the Space Station

Dragon is a free-flying spacecraft designed to deliver both cargo and people to orbiting destinations. Dragon made history in 2012 when it became the first commercial spacecraft in history to deliver cargo to the International Space Station and safely return cargo to Earth, a feat previously achieved only by governments.

It is the only spacecraft currently flying that is capable of returning significant amounts of cargo to Earth. Currently, Dragon carries cargo to space, but it was designed from the beginning to carry humans. Under an agreement with NASA, SpaceX is now developing the refinements that will enable Dragon to fly crew. Dragon's first manned test flight is expected to take place as early as 2018.

Cargo

Resupply the Space Station

  • DELIVERY AND RETURN SERVICES: Dragon is the first commercial spacecraft to deliver cargo to the International Space Station and is currently the only spacecraft capable of returning significant amounts of cargo to Earth.
  • VERSATILE CARGO RACKS: The racks are a honeycomb carbon-aluminum construction designed for efficient packing in a zero-gravity environment. They accommodate various standard-size NASA cargo bags and freezers for biological samples.

Crew

Crew Cabin

  • PARTNERSHIP WITH NASA: Dragon was designed from the outset to fly humans to space. Under an agreement with NASA, SpaceX is upgrading Dragon to allow for crew-carrying capability.
  • CREW UPGRADES: Dragon will be the world’s safest crew transport vehicle, with seating for seven, life-support systems, manual controls, and a powered launch escape system.
  • FIRST LAUNCH: Upgrades to Dragon are in progress. Dragon's first manned test flight is expected to take place as early as 2018.

Dragon Lab

A Free-Flying Microgravity Laboratory

  • FACILITATING RESEARCH: DragonLab is an unmanned platform designed for research and testing in a microgravity environment independent of the ISS.
  • LONGEVITY: DragonLab can transport payloads, experiments, and instruments for missions ranging from one week to two years.
  • CAPABILITIES: DragonLab supports space physics, relativity experiments, radiation effects research, and more.

Background

SpaceX designs, manufactures, and launches advanced rockets and spacecraft. Founded in 2002, SpaceX aims to revolutionize space technology and ultimately enable humans to live on other planets.

The Dragon spacecraft is capable of carrying up to 7 passengers to and from Earth orbit, and it is the first private spacecraft to take humans to the space station.
